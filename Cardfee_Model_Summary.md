# Card Fee Model Summary

| Section | Model Summary |
| --- | --- |
| **Development Data** | - Monthly card fee revenue through December 2025.<br>- Commercial and Small Business Card and Consumer Card use January 2011 through September 2023 for development and October 2023 through December 2025 for out-of-time assessment.<br>- The Commercial and Small Business Card forecast uses supplied CPI and Corporate Profits scenario paths through March 2028.<br>- Debit Card excludes calendar year 2011 because of the October 2011 Durbin Amendment impact. Confirmed March and December 2012 one-time reversals are replaced with the average of adjacent months; the adopted quarterly calibration uses data through 2025 Q4.<br>- Macroeconomic variables and scenario paths are sourced from Moody's Analytics where applicable. |
| **Segmentation** | - **Commercial and Small Business Card:** Commercial Card plus Small Business fee revenue; Sponsorships and QualPay are excluded.<br>- **Consumer Card:** Consumer Card fee revenue.<br>- **Debit Card:** Debit Card fee revenue. |
| **Methodology** | **1. Commercial and Small Business Card:** Two-step error-correction model combining a CPI-based long-run relationship with an overlay-adjusted short-run model using revenue lags and Corporate Profits; forecasts are generated recursively under supplied macroeconomic scenarios.<br><br>**2. Consumer Card:** Monthly log-change autoregressive model using two revenue lags, January and December seasonal indicators, and three-month smoothed Advance Retail Sales growth.<br><br>**3. Debit Card:** No tested macro regression passed the diagnostic requirements; the adopted treatment uses a trailing eight-quarter run-rate baseline and a post-2015 mean-minus-two-standard-deviations stress floor. |
