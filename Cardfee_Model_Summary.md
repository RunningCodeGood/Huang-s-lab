# Card Fee Model Summary

| Section | Model Summary |
| --- | --- |
| **Development Data** | - Monthly card fee revenue through December 2025.<br>- Commercial and Small Business Card and Consumer Card use January 2011 through September 2023 for development and October 2023 through December 2025 for out-of-time assessment.<br>- The Commercial and Small Business Card forecast uses supplied CPI and Corporate Profits scenario paths through March 2028.<br>- Debit Card excludes calendar year 2011 because of the October 2011 Durbin Amendment impact. Confirmed March and December 2012 one-time reversals are replaced with the average of adjacent months; the adopted quarterly calibration uses data through 2025 Q4.<br>- Macroeconomic variables and scenario paths are sourced from Moody's Analytics where applicable. |
| **Segmentation** | - **Commercial and Small Business Card:** Commercial Card plus Small Business fee revenue; Sponsorships and QualPay are excluded.<br>- **Consumer Card:** Consumer Card fee revenue.<br>- **Debit Card:** Debit Card fee revenue. |
| **Methodology** | **1. Commercial and Small Business Card**<br>- The short-run target is the 12-month percentage change in combined fee revenue.<br>- A two-step Engle-Granger error-correction framework is used. The long-run level is modeled with CPI; the residual is scaled by the fitted long-run level, adjusted for the realized October 2023 to December 2025 trend overlay, and held flat after December 2025.<br>- The short-run model includes the adjusted error-correction term at lag 12, revenue-growth lags 1 and 6, and Corporate Profits at lag 2. Forecasts are generated recursively and are conditional on supplied CPI and Corporate Profits paths.<br>- Because the out-of-time period informs both the macro screen and the overlay, its reported errors are descriptive rather than an untouched validation result.<br><br>**2. Consumer Card**<br>- Revenue is modeled as monthly log change.<br>- The model combines autoregressive terms at lags 1 and 2, January and December seasonal indicators, and a three-month trailing moving average of month-over-month Advance Retail Sales growth.<br>- The fitted growth rate is converted back to fee-revenue levels for accuracy assessment. Structural tests and rolling estimates support stability through the out-of-time period.<br><br>**3. Debit Card**<br>- Level, year-over-year error-correction, month-over-month ARDL, and quarter-over-quarter ARDL specifications were tested with macroeconomic candidates and lags; none passed the full significance, autocorrelation, normality, and multicollinearity gates.<br>- The series contains an unexplained, sustained level shift beginning in 2015 Q1, so the adopted treatment does not use a fitted macro regression.<br>- Baseline revenue is the trailing eight-quarter average through 2025 Q4, or 4,747.6 per quarter.<br>- The stress floor is the post-2015 mean minus two standard deviations, or 3,815.9 per quarter, approximately 1,272.0 per month and 19.6% below the baseline. |
