Commercial + Small Business Card Fee Overview

METHODOLOGY

Target variable:
Combined fee revenue = Commercial Card + Small Business.
The short-run model forecasts its YoY growth:
y_t = 100 * (Combined_t / Combined_(t-12) - 1)

Engle-Granger two-step approach:

Long-run:
Estimate combined fee revenue as a function of CPI using in-sample data, and test residual stationarity.

Error correction and overlay:
Scale the long-run residual by the contemporaneous fitted level. Subtract a linear trend estimated over Oct 2023-Dec 2025 to obtain the overlay-adjusted EC.

Short-run model:
Apply IS/OOT variable screening and forward selection. The final model includes adjusted EC at lag 12, own YoY growth at lags 1 and 6, and corporate profit YoY growth at lag 2. Forecast recursively from Jan 2026 to Mar 2028 under S1 and S4 macro paths.

ASSUMPTIONS

- Model relationships remain stable over the forecast horizon.
- The overlay stays at its Dec 2025 level of -30.143 percentage points.

LIMITATIONS & WEAKNESSES

- The CPI-based long-run relationship and fixed overlay may miss future structural changes.
