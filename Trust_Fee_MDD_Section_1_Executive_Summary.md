# 1. Executive Summary

The Trust Fee Revenue Model is used to project Trust Fee revenue under baseline and stress scenarios as part of the Bank's annual Comprehensive Capital Analysis and Review (CCAR) and Pre-Provision Net Revenue (PPNR) forecasting process. The model covers revenue generated from Trust assets under management (AUM), assets under custody (AUC), and Asset Management Income.

The Trust AUM component consists of three business segments: TFO (family office), IT (institutional trust), and PT (private trust). Revenue for each segment is projected by applying a fee rate to the corresponding projected AUM balance. Trust AUC revenue is projected by applying a blended AUC fee rate to the projected aggregate AUC balance. Asset Management Income is projected using an Asset Management Income yield applied to projected IA balances.

The model is primarily a qualitative forecasting framework that combines observed Trust business metrics with judgmental assumptions regarding balance growth, fee rates, market sensitivity, and stress behavior. Baseline AUM and AUC balances are projected using recent historical trends. Stress balances are projected using a defined market-stress decline path. IA balances are projected based on changes in the applicable S&P 500 scenario, while the Asset Management Income yield is held constant across the forecast horizon.

Key model inputs include historical AUM and AUC balances, historical fee rates, IA balances, Asset Management Income yields, and applicable baseline and stress scenario paths. Key assumptions include fixed fee rates, trend-based baseline balance projections, a shared stress decline methodology for AUM and AUC balances, and a stable Asset Management Income yield. The principal outputs are quarterly baseline and stress revenue projections over the nine-quarter forecast horizon for Trust AUM fees, Trust AUC fees, Asset Management Income, and total Trust Fee revenue.
